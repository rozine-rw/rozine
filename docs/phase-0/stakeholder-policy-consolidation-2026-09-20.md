### Fee confirmation and arithmetic

The screenshot shows Robert replying "Yes, exactly" to the explicit 0.7% combined / 0.35%-each question, then confirming the individual rates, settled-gross basis and buyer-plus/seller-minus flow. It also contains the isolated phrase "70% combined". Preserve that transcription discrepancy; the explicit component rates and quoted question support 0.7%, not a 70% charge. No additional fee clarification is required.

For gross consideration G, each side's fee is half-up-rounded `G × 35 / 10,000` RWF. Buyer debit is G plus buyer fee; seller credit is G minus seller fee; platform receipt is the sum of the two rounded fees. These are derived synthetic examples, not executed trades or proof of a settlement engine.

| Gross RWF | Buyer fee RWF | Buyer debit RWF | Seller fee RWF | Seller credit RWF | Platform fee RWF |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1000 | 4 | 1004 | 4 | 996 | 8 |
| 10000 | 35 | 10035 | 35 | 9965 | 70 |
| 20000 | 70 | 20070 | 70 | 19930 | 140 |

Whole-RWF rounding means the realized combined fee on a small trade can differ from exactly 0.7% of gross. At RWF 1,000, the two RWF 3.50 components round to RWF 4 each. The gross threshold is applied before the seller fee; a seller receipt below RWF 1,000 does not itself make the gross trade undersized. No minimum fee has been invented.
