Rating 0–5
How safe the business looks. 4.0+ is Strong, 3.0+ Stable, below 2.0 Distressed.
Years trading + sector + profit margin, scored 40–92 and shown out of 5.
Yield
What you earn on the money if the note repays in full.
10% base, plus a premium for weaker ratings and longer terms. Capped at 15%.
Cover (DSCR)
How much room the business has to pay you. 1.25× means it earns 25% more than the payment.
Monthly profit ÷ monthly repayment. No note is issued below 1.25×.
Capacity
The most that business can borrow — the ceiling on the note size.
Sized off monthly profit at 1.25× cover, never more than 35% of annual revenue.
