2.1. **Eligibility.** You may open an account as an individual investor or as an institutional investor. You choose which when you sign up.

## 3. Investing, Rozine Plus and cart checkout

3.1. **SME Loan Notes.** When you commit funds to a campaign on Rozine, you buy a fractional debt instrument ("Note") issued directly by the borrowing small or medium enterprise ("Business" or "Issuer"). Notes are sold in units of RWF 5,000.

3.2. **Rozine Plus tiers.** Rozine's fee on your earnings depends on your Total Active Capital Deployed across all live Notes in your portfolio at the moment of checkout:

| Tier | Total Active Capital Deployed | Fee on Earnings |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | below RWF 1,000,000 | 15% |
| Plus | RWF 1,000,000 to RWF 4,999,999 | 12% |
| Gold | RWF 5,000,000 to RWF 24,999,999 | 10% |
| Platinum | RWF 25,000,000 to RWF 99,999,999 | 9% |
| Diamond | RWF 100,000,000 and above | 8% |

3.3. **Your net rate.** The interest rate the Business pays is passed to investors in full. Your net rate on a Note is that rate less your tier's fee: Net Investor Rate = Business Rate × (1 − Fee on Earnings). Rozine keeps no other interest spread.

3.4. **Cart checkout.** You may combine several Notes into one cart and check them out in a single order. Rozine works out your Total Active Capital Deployed after the order by adding the total value of the cart to your current active capital. If that total crosses into a higher tier, the lower fee applies to every Note in that cart.

3.5. **Fee locked per Note.** The fee rate quoted at checkout is locked into each Note for its whole term. Later changes to your active capital, up or down, do not change the rate on Notes you already hold.

3.6. **What the fee applies to.** The fee applies only to the return (interest) part of each payout. Principal repayments are returned to your wallet in full, with no fee deducted.

3.7. **A confirmed purchase is final.** Once you confirm a checkout, your funds are committed to the campaign and cannot be withdrawn from it. If Rozine cancels a campaign, or it closes without raising its target, Rozine returns your committed principal to your wallet in full.

## 4. Investment limits

4.1. **Single-investor cap.** One investor may fund at most 50% of any Business's target raise. For example, an investor can fund at most RWF 50,000,000 of a RWF 100,000,000 raise.

4.2. **No other caps.** There is no other limit on how much you invest or in how many Businesses, for individual or institutional investors.

## 5. Repayments, arrears and late fees

5.1. **Repayment schedule.** Businesses repay monthly. Repayments are distributed pro rata to Note holders once collected.

5.2. **Late fees.** If a Business does not pay an instalment on its due date, a late fee is added in steps: +5% on the due date, a further +5% on day 7 and a further +5% on day 30. Each step is calculated on the overdue instalment (principal plus return), up to a total of 15% per overdue instalment. Partial payments reduce the amount used for later steps.

5.3. **Late fees are passed to you.** All collected late fees are passed to the affected investors pro rata. Rozine takes no fee on collected late fees.

5.4. **Not guaranteed.** Late fees and recovered debts are paid to you only when they are actually collected from the Business. They are not guaranteed, insured or underwritten by Rozine Technologies Ltd.

## 6. Wallet withdrawals and provider fees

6.1. **Limits.** The minimum wallet withdrawal is RWF 1,000. The maximum per withdrawal is RWF 2,000,000.

6.2. **Processing time.** Withdrawals are paid through payment service providers (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or bank transfer) and are processed within 1 to 24 hours.

6.3. **Provider fees.** Fees charged by the mobile network or bank are shown before you confirm a withdrawal and are deducted from the amount withdrawn. Rozine does not add its own withdrawal fee.
