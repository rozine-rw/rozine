1.3. **Rozine is a platform, not a lender.** Rozine connects businesses and investors and runs the note. Rozine never lends its own money, guarantees capital or returns, buys or sells notes, makes a market or sets resale prices.

Your wallet balance and your notes are not insured by the Government of Rwanda, the National Bank of Rwanda (BNR) or the CMA.

1.5. **Your money is kept separate.** All customer money is held in a segregated client account at a licensed Rwandan commercial bank, separate from Rozine's own money. Only Rozine's earned, disclosed fees leave that account for Rozine.

## 2. Who can invest, and verification

2.1. **Eligibility.** You may invest as a verified individual, an accredited investor or a corporate treasury.
- An individual must be at least 18 years old and resident in Rwanda while Rozine is in the sandbox.
- An accredited investor or corporate treasury must be a legal entity. Its entity documents are verified in the same way as a Business's, and it names an authorised signatory.

2.2. **Identity verification.** Individuals verify their identity with a Rwandan National ID or a passport.

2.3. **Your own account.** You must link at least one bank or Mobile Money account held in your own name. Deposits come only from, and withdrawals go only to, your own verified accounts. A deposit from someone else's account is held and reviewed by Compliance.

2.4. **Screening.** You authorise Rozine to screen you against UN, OFAC and EU sanctions lists and politically-exposed-person lists before your wallet is activated, and periodically after that. A possible match blocks activation until Compliance clears it.

2.5. **Before verification.** Until you are verified, you can browse but cannot fund a wallet, invest or trade.

## 4. Notes and raises

4.1. **What you buy.** A note is a fixed-return debt issued by one business. The business owes each holder directly, in proportion to the units they hold. A unit is RWF 5,000 of principal. Units are recorded on Rozine's ledger.

4.2. **Return and schedule.** Each note pays a fixed total return of 10.0% to 15.0% of principal for its whole term, in equal monthly instalments over 3, 4, 5 or 6 months. The full schedule is shown before you commit.

4.3. **Raises.** A raise is open for 30 days or until it is 100% funded. If it is not fully funded after 30 days, every commitment is returned to investors' wallets in full, and no fee is charged to anyone.

4.4. **Early repayment.** A business may repay early. You still receive the full return you were promised, less the earnings fee on the return.

## 5. Investing through the cart

5.1. **Units and limits.** Every investment is a whole number of RWF 5,000 units. One investor may hold at most 50% of any raise, counting units bought in the raise and on the resale market together. An investment cannot exceed what is left to fill.

5.2. **Cart checkout.** You may put several notes in a cart and buy them in one checkout. A checkout is all or nothing: if any line fails when it is executed, none is committed, and you are told which line failed and why.

5.3. **Funding a checkout.** You can pay from your wallet, Mobile Money or bank. Money from Mobile Money or bank must reach your wallet before the commitment is confirmed.

5.4. **What you see first.** Before you confirm, you see the amount, every fee in RWF, the net return, the full schedule and the risk warning. If anything changes before you confirm, the confirmation is blocked and the figures refresh.

5.5. **A confirmed commitment is final.** You cannot cancel a confirmed commitment. Money comes back to you only if the raise does not fill.

## 6. Fees

6.1. **Earnings fee.** Rozine charges a fee only on the return part of each payout, never on principal. The rate depends on your active capital after checkout:

| Active capital after checkout | Earnings fee |
|---|---|
| Under RWF 1,000,000 | 15% |
| RWF 1,000,000 to under RWF 5,000,000 | 12% |
| RWF 5,000,000 to under RWF 25,000,000 | 10% |
| RWF 25,000,000 to under RWF 100,000,000 | 9% |
| RWF 100,000,000 and above | 8% |

6.2. **Active capital.** Your active capital is the principal outstanding in the notes you hold, plus the total of the checkout. Wallet cash never counts.

6.3. **Rate locked per investment.** The rate is set once, at checkout. Every line in that checkout locks the same rate for the life of that investment. Earlier investments keep the rate they locked.

6.4. **Resale fee.** When you sell units on the resale market, Rozine charges you 2% of the sale price. Buyers pay no fee. There is no fee for listing, reserving, cancelling or letting a listing expire.

6.5. **Nothing else.** Rozine earns no interest spread, no fee on returned principal, no share of late charges and nothing from a default.

## 7. Payouts, late payments and default

7.1. **Payouts.** At 23:59 on each due date, the instalment is collected from the business's wallet. Your share is credited to your wallet at that moment, less the earnings fee at your locked rate.

7.2. **Late payments.** If a business pays only part of an instalment, what it paid is passed to holders, and any money that later reaches its wallet is collected immediately. A late charge of 5% of the unpaid part is added on the due date, on day 7 and on day 30, up to 15% of the instalment. Every franc of late charges collected goes to that note's holders. Rozine takes nothing.

7.3. **Default.** If arrears are not fully cleared by day 31, the note is in default and legal recovery begins. Everything recovered goes to holders in proportion to their units. Rozine does not cover shortfalls. A defaulted note can never be traded again.

7.4. **Not guaranteed.** Late charges and recoveries are paid only when they are actually collected from the business.

## 8. Selling on the resale market

8.1. **What can be sold.** You may list units of a current note: one that is not late, in default or disputed. You choose how many whole units to sell and a whole-franc price per unit. A listing lasts 7 days.

8.2. **Price cap.** You cannot ask more per unit than the remaining contractual payouts per unit. Rozine sets no price.

8.3. **Buying.** A buyer may buy some or all of a listing. Starting a purchase reserves the units for 5 minutes, using money already in the buyer's wallet. While a reservation is open, the seller cannot cancel or reprice.

8.4. **Settlement.** Money and units change hands in one step. Ownership passes at settlement. The buyer's units carry the buyer's own earnings-fee rate, set at settlement.

8.5. **Pauses and halts.** Trading in a note pauses from 23:00 on its due date until that month's payouts are posted. Compliance may halt the whole market or one note, with a recorded reason. A halt cancels open listings.

8.6. **Market conduct.** Rozine monitors for market abuse, such as trading between linked accounts, trading on unpublished information, or using several accounts to get around the 50% limit. Rozine may investigate and act, and reports suspicious activity to the Financial Intelligence Centre as the law requires.

## 9. Your wallet

9.1. **Withdrawals.** Each withdrawal is between RWF 1,000 and RWF 2,000,000, paid only to your own verified account. Rozine charges no withdrawal fee. The provider's actual charge is shown before you confirm. Unusual requests may be held for up to 24 hours for review.

9.2. **Committed money.** Money committed to an open raise or held by a resale reservation cannot be withdrawn.

## 10. If something goes wrong

10.1. **Rozine's own mistakes.** If Rozine causes you a loss, for example through a ledger error or staff misconduct, Rozine compensates you in full from its own funds. Losses from a business defaulting are the disclosed risk and are not compensated.

10.2. **If Rozine stops operating.** Rozine stops new business, notifies you and the CMA, and refunds every commitment in unfunded raises in full. Issued notes continue to be serviced to maturity. You can withdraw your whole wallet balance free of charge.

10.3. **Complaints.** You can complain in the app, by e-mail to info@rozine.rw or on WhatsApp Business. You get a reference number, an acknowledgement within 4 hours and a resolution within 48 hours, or an explanation of why it will take longer and when to expect it. You may take your complaint to the CMA at any stage.

10.4. **Changes to these Terms.** Rozine tells you before any change of terms takes effect. A change never alters the terms of a note you already hold.

10.5. **Limitation of liability.** Apart from section 10.1, and to the maximum extent permitted under Rwandan law, Rozine is not liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages, or for investment losses caused by business default, business failure or force majeure.
