# Rozine Privacy Note

- **Document identifier:** `privacy-note-2026-10-13`
- **Replaces:** `privacy-note-2026-10-01` (draft, never loaded)
- **Effective date:** 13 October 2026
- **Applies to:** every user of the Investor app, Business app, Auditor app and Admin console
- **Issued by:** Rozine Technologies Ltd (TIN: 156642791)

## 1. Who we are and the law we follow

Rozine Technologies Ltd ("Rozine") processes personal data in compliance with Law N° 058/2021 of 13/10/2021 Relating to the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy of the Republic of Rwanda. Rozine decides how and why your personal data is processed. Rozine is applying to the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) for registration as a data controller and data processor.

## 2. What we collect

2.1. **Identity and KYC data.** Your full legal name, National ID number or passport details, date of birth and residential address, and the identity documents you upload. For an institutional investor or a Business, we also collect its official company name, its Tax Identification Number (TIN), and the full name and address of its representative.

2.2. **Financial and transaction data.** Mobile Money numbers (MTN or Airtel), bank account details, transaction records, wallet balances, investment portfolios and repayment history.

2.3. **Technical data.** IP address, device and browser details, and login and security logs.

## 3. Why we use it

We process your personal data on these lawful bases under Law N° 058/2021:
- **Performing our contract with you:** to open your account, process your investments, handle wallet deposits and withdrawals, and collect repayments.
- **Legal and regulatory obligations:** to verify identity (KYC), apply investment limits, and prevent money laundering (AML).
- **Legitimate interest:** to keep the platform secure and prevent fraud.

## 4. Where your data is stored

4.1. Rozine stores your personal, financial and transaction data on a server that Rozine rents from Contabo GmbH, in a data centre in Germany, in the European Union. This includes your account and identity details, the documents you upload, your wallet and transaction records, and security logs. Your data is not currently stored in Rwanda, and Rozine does not keep copies of it anywhere else.

4.2. When Rozine sends you an email, your name, email address and the content of that email are passed to our email provider, Resend, Inc., which processes them in the United States to deliver the message.

4.3. Data travelling between your device, our server and our email provider is encrypted in transit. Rozine is applying to the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) for the registration that Article 50 of Law N° 058/2021 requires to store personal data outside Rwanda.

## 5. Who we share it with

Rozine does not sell, trade or rent personal data. We share personal data only with:
- **Regulators:** the Capital Market Authority (CMA), the National Bank of Rwanda (BNR) and the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA), where the law requires it.
- **Payment service providers:** MTN Rwanda, Airtel Rwanda and commercial banks, to carry out wallet transfers.
- **Our email provider:** Resend, Inc., as described in section 4.2.

## 6. Your rights

Under Law N° 058/2021 you have these rights:
- **Access and correction:** to see your personal data and have incomplete or inaccurate data corrected.
- **Deletion:** to ask for your personal data to be deleted, subject to the record-keeping periods that Rwandan financial and tax law require.
- **Withdrawing consent:** to withdraw consent to marketing messages at any time.

Requests regarding data subject rights may be submitted to info@rozine.rw.
