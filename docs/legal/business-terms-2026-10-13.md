## 1. Who we are and what Rozine is

1.1. **The agreement.** These Terms are a legally binding agreement between the business applying to raise ("Business" or "Issuer") and Rozine Technologies Ltd ("Rozine"), a private limited liability company incorporated under the laws of the Republic of Rwanda (TIN: 156642791).

1.2. **The CMA sandbox.** Rozine is testing this service in the Capital Market Authority's Fintech Regulatory Sandbox, under limits set by the CMA. Admission to the sandbox is not a licence or an endorsement. Rozine has applied for admission, and that application is pending. Rozine will update these Terms when the CMA decides.

1.3. **Rozine is a platform, not a lender.** Rozine connects businesses and investors and runs the note. Rozine never lends its own money. The Business owes each investor directly.

## 2. Eligibility and verification

2.1. **Who may apply.** The Business must be registered in Rwanda, have verified annual revenue of RWF 30,000,000 or more, and upload 36 months of bank and Mobile Money statements. A repeat issuer whose previous Rozine note was repaid on time needs 12 months.

2.2. **Verification (KYB).** The Business provides its RDB registration, its Tax Identification Number (TIN), its Articles of Association, its shareholder register, its directors' IDs and the identity of its beneficial owners. Directors and beneficial owners are screened against sanctions and politically-exposed-person lists.

2.3. **Official representative.** One official representative acts for the Business for every approval, acceptance and dispute. A second signatory is never required. The person accepting these Terms confirms they are that representative and can bind the Business.

2.4. **The Business's own account.** The Business links a bank or Mobile Money account in its own registered name. Disbursements and withdrawals go only to that account.

## 3. Assessment and offer

3.1. **Formulas, not judgement.** Rozine's engine decides the rating, the offer and the return from a published formula and the verified figures. No staff member can override a failed check.

3.2. **How much the Business can raise.** The offer is the lowest of: the amount requested, the amount the Business's cash flow can carry at a coverage of 1.50, 35% of its verified annual revenue, and RWF 100,000,000. It is rounded down to a multiple of RWF 5,000. An application whose coverage is below 1.25, or whose offer would be below RWF 3,000,000, is rejected with the reason.

3.3. **Return and term.** The note carries a fixed total return of 10.0% to 15.0% of principal, set by the rating and the term of 3, 4, 5 or 6 months. Interest is charged on the full principal raised.

3.4. **Changes before acceptance.** If verified facts change before the Business accepts, the offer is withdrawn and recalculated. Once a note is issued, its terms never change.

## 4. Verification by an Audit Partner

4.1. **Baseline visit.** Before the note is listed, an independent ICPAR-licensed Audit Partner visits the Business and seals a baseline report. The report is published on the listing.

4.2. **Every month.** While the note is outstanding, the Business uploads the month's statements and records by 10 days before each due date. The Audit Partner verifies them in person and seals a monthly report. The report is published to investors before the due date.

4.3. **Approving or disputing a report.** After a report is sealed, the official representative has 24 hours to approve or dispute it. No response within 24 hours counts as approval. A dispute is reviewed by Rozine, and the decision and its reason are published with the report.

4.4. **Unannounced visits.** Rozine may send an Audit Partner for an unannounced Flash Audit. The Business must give the Audit Partner access.

## 5. The raise and disbursement

5.1. **The raise.** The note is offered to investors for 30 days or until it is 100% funded. If it is not fully funded after 30 days, every investor is refunded in full and no fee is charged.

5.2. **Disbursement.** When the raise reaches 100%, the money is disbursed automatically to the Business's verified account, less the platform fee.

5.3. **Fees.** The Business pays one fee: a platform fee of 3% of the principal raised, deducted at disbursement. No application fee is charged during the sandbox. No fee is charged on repayments.

Example: on a RWF 12,000,000 note at an 11.1% return over 6 months, the platform fee is RWF 360,000. The Business receives RWF 11,640,000 and repays RWF 13,332,000.

## 6. Repayment

6.1. **Obligation to repay.** The Business must repay the principal plus the fixed return in equal monthly instalments: principal × (1 + total return) ÷ number of months. The first instalment is due one month after disbursement.

6.2. **Automatic collection.** The Business authorises Rozine to collect each instalment from its Rozine wallet at 23:59 on the due date. Rozine sends reminders to fund the wallet from 14 days before each due date.

6.3. **Short wallet.** If the wallet holds less than the instalment, what is there is collected and the rest becomes overdue. Any money that later reaches the wallet is collected the moment it arrives.

6.4. **Early repayment.** The Business may repay the whole remaining balance early. The return owed does not reduce.

## 7. Arrears and default

7.1. **Late charges.** A late charge of 5% of the unpaid part of the instalment is added on the due date, on day 7 and on day 30, up to 15% of the instalment. All late charges go to the note's investors.

7.2. **Escalation.** The Business receives daily reminders from the first day late. On day 7, Rozine contacts the Business and its Audit Partner visits. On day 30, a formal legal demand is sent.

7.3. **Cure.** If the Business pays all arrears and late charges before day 31, the note returns to normal automatically. Dates and amounts are never rescheduled.

7.4. **Default.** If arrears are not fully cleared by day 31, the note is in default. Legal counsel starts recovery under Rwandan commercial law, and automatic collection from the Business's wallet continues.

## 8. General

8.1. **Complaints.** The Business can complain in the app, by e-mail to info@rozine.rw or on WhatsApp Business. It gets a reference number, an acknowledgement within 4 hours and a resolution within 48 hours, or an explanation of why it will take longer and when to expect it. It may take its complaint to the CMA at any stage.

8.2. **Changes to these Terms.** Rozine tells the Business before any change of terms takes effect. A change never alters the terms of a note already issued.
