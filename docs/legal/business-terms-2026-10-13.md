# Rozine Business Terms and Conditions

- **Document identifier:** `business-terms-2026-10-13`
- **Replaces:** `business-terms-2026-10-01` (draft, never loaded)
- **Effective date:** 13 October 2026
- **Applies to:** every registered company and SME that applies to raise on the Rozine Business app
- **Issued by:** Rozine Technologies Ltd (TIN: 156642791)

## 1. Eligibility and representations

1.1. **Registration.** The borrowing entity ("Business" or "Issuer") confirms that it is a lawfully registered legal entity in Rwanda with a valid Tax Identification Number (TIN) issued by the Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA).

1.2. **Authority to sign.** The person accepting these Terms confirms that they are an official manager, official representative or major shareholder of the Business, with full authority to bind it to debt obligations. One such person may sign the application and approve the Business's monthly audit reports. They may invite others to take part through the Business app.

1.3. **Bank account for disbursement.** The bank account that receives money raised on Rozine must be in the Business's name and require at least two signatories. The Business shows the account documentation, including its signatories, to its auditor during the audit.

## 2. Fees and disbursement

2.1. **Application fee.** There is no application fee during the MVP pilot. After the pilot, a non-refundable application fee of RWF 50,000 will be charged when the application is approved after the audit. Rozine will update these Terms before that fee applies.

2.2. **Platform service fee.** When a campaign raise is fully filled, Rozine charges a platform service fee of 2.0% of the principal raised. It is taken once, at disbursement. No fee is charged on monthly repayments.

2.3. **Net disbursement.** The Business receives the principal raised less the platform service fee:
Net Disbursement = Principal Raised − (2.0% × Principal Raised).

2.4. **Interest.** Interest is charged on the full principal raised, not on the net amount disbursed.

## 3. Repayment and automatic collection

3.1. **Obligation to repay.** The Business must repay the full principal plus the agreed flat interest according to its repayment schedule.

3.2. **Automatic wallet debit.** The Business authorises Rozine to debit its Rozine wallet automatically at 23:59 CAT on each instalment due date.

3.3. **Retries.** If the wallet does not hold enough funds on the due date, Rozine retries the debit once a day from day 1 to day 7 after the due date.

3.4. **Partial and early payments.** The Business may pay part of an instalment. A partial payment immediately reduces the overdue amount used for later late-fee steps. The Business may also pay future instalments early, at any time, with no penalty and no discount on the interest.

## 4. Arrears and default

4.1. **Late fees.** An overdue instalment attracts late fees, added to what the Business owes:
- On the due date (23:59 CAT): +5% of the overdue instalment.
- Day 7: a further +5% of the amount still overdue.
- Day 30: a further +5% of the amount still overdue.

Late fees are capped at 15% in total per instalment.

4.2. **Default.** An instalment still unpaid on day 31 after its due date is a material event of default. Rozine may then begin formal legal recovery against the Business.
