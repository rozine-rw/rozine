1. BORROWER ELIGIBILITY AND REPRESENTATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1. Incorporation: The borrowing entity ("Business" or "Issuer") warrants that 
it is a lawfully registered legal entity in Rwanda holding a valid Tax Identification 
Number (TIN) issued by the Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA).

1.2. Authority: The individual executing this agreement represents that they 
hold full corporate authority to bind the Business to debt obligations.

1.3. Credit Bureau Authorization: The Business authorizes Rozine to query its 
credit history with licensed Credit Reference Bureaus (CRBs) in Rwanda and to 
report repayment performance, delays, or defaults to relevant credit bureaus.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. APPLICATION FEES AND DISBURSEMENT DEDUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1. Upfront Application Fee: A non-refundable Application Fee of RWF 50,000 is 
due upon submission of a campaign application for credit assessment, underwriting, 
and risk scoring.

2.2. Platform Service Fee: Upon successful completion of a campaign raise on the 
Rozine platform, Rozine charges a 2.0% Platform Service Fee calculated against 
the gross target principal raised.

2.3. Net Disbursement Formula: Disbursements to the Business's designated bank 
account or mobile money wallet are executed net of platform fees:
   Net Disbursement = Target Raised Principal - (2.0% x Target Principal) - Application Fee

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. REPAYMENT OBLIGATIONS AND AUTOMATED COLLECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1. Unconditional Obligation: The Business undertakes an unconditional 
obligation to repay the full principal amount plus agreed flat interest according 
to the six-month (or specified) repayment schedule.

3.2. Mandatory Wallet Auto-Debit: The Business authorizes Rozine to automatically 
debit its platform wallet on 23:59 CAT of each scheduled instalment due date.

3.3. Auto-Collection Retry Protocol: If available wallet funds are insufficient 
on the due date, Rozine's automated collection engine will execute daily auto-debit 
retries against the Business's account from Day 1 through Day 7 post-due-date.

3.4. Partial and Early Payments: Partial instalment payments are permitted and 
will immediately reduce the outstanding balance used to calculate subsequent 
late-fee escalation steps. Early payment of future instalments is permitted at 
any time without penalty or yield discount.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ARREARS MANAGEMENT AND DEFAULT CONSEQUENCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1. Late Fee Escalation: Overdue instalments attract late penalty fees added 
to the Business's debt balance:
  - Day 0 (Due Date 23:59 CAT): +5% penalty on overdue instalment.
  - Day 7: Additional +5% penalty on remaining overdue balance.
  - Day 30: Additional +5% penalty on remaining overdue balance (capped at 15% 
    total per instalment).

4.2. Default Classification (Day 45): Non-payment exceeding 45 calendar days 
constitutes a material event of default. Rozine reserves the right to initiate 
formal legal recovery proceedings, enforce collateral/guarantees, report default 
status to RRA/CRB authorities, and transition the recovery process to third-party 
recovery agencies or the First-Loss Reserve Fund framework under CMA Sandbox rules.
