3. INVESTMENT MECHANICS, PLUS TIERS, AND CART CHECKOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1. SME Loan Notes: When you commit funds to a campaign on Rozine, you are 
purchasing a fractionalized, bilateral debt instrument ("Note") issued directly 
by the borrowing Small and Medium Enterprise ("Business" or "Issuer").

3.2. Rozine Plus Tier Architecture: Your eligibility for discounted platform 
service fees is governed by your Total Active Capital Deployed across all live 
Notes in your portfolio at the moment of checkout, according to the following 
thresholds:
  - Standard Tier (< RWF 1,000,000 active): 10.0% Fee on Earnings
  - Plus Tier (RWF 1,000,000 – RWF 4,999,999 active): 8.0% Fee on Earnings
  - Gold Tier (RWF 5,000,000 – RWF 24,999,999 active): 6.5% Fee on Earnings
  - Platinum Tier (RWF 25,000,000 – RWF 99,999,999 active): 5.0% Fee on Earnings
  - Diamond Tier (>= RWF 100,000,000 active): 4.0% Fee on Earnings

3.3. Cart / Basket Checkout Engine: Investors may aggregate allocations across 
multiple active Notes into a single cart checkout order. The system evaluates 
your post-commitment Active Capital Deployed by combining your current active 
balance with the total value of all Notes in the cart. If the aggregate order 
pushes your active capital across a higher tier threshold, the lower fee rate 
applies to every Note contained within that cart execution.

3.4. Note-Level Fee Locking: The investor fee rate evaluated and quoted at 
checkout is permanently locked into that specific Note record for its entire 
tenure. Subsequent changes to your total active portfolio balance, tier upgrades, 
or tier downgrades will not alter the locked fee rate on previously executed Notes.

3.5. Fee Application Scope: Rozine's investor service fee applies strictly to 
the return (interest) portion of each payout received ("Fee on Earnings"). 
Principal repayments are returned to your wallet 100% gross without any fee 
deduction.

5. REPAYMENTS, ARREARS, AND LATE FEE PASSTHROUGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1. Repayment Schedule: SME repayments are collected monthly and distributed 
pro-rata to Note holders upon collection.

5.2. N4 Escalation Late Fee Engine: If an SME fails to pay an instalment on 
the due date, the system applies a structured late fee ladder (+5% at due date, 
+5% on Day 7, and +5% on Day 30) calculated on the overdue instalment amount 
(principal + return), capped at a cumulative maximum of 15% per overdue instalment. 
Partial repayments reduce the base for subsequent late fee steps.

5.3. 100% Late Fee Passthrough: 100% of all collected late fees are passed 
through directly to affected investors on a pro-rata basis. Rozine charges 0% 
platform fee on collected late fees.

5.4. Non-Guarantee & Pass-Through Disclaimer: Late fee distributions and debt 
recoveries are payable to you strictly upon actual collection from the borrowing 
Business. Late fee distributions and principal recoveries are not guaranteed, 
insured, or underwritten by Rozine Technologies Ltd.
