1. STATUTORY COMPLIANCE DECLARATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rozine Technologies Ltd is committed to protecting user privacy and processes 
all personal data in strict compliance with Law N° 058/2021 of 13/10/2021 
Relating to the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy of the Republic of Rwanda. 
Rozine acts as a registered Data Controller and Data Processor under the oversight 
of the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CATEGORIES OF DATA COLLECTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1. Identity & KYC Data: Full legal name, National Identity Number (NIN), 
Passport details, date of birth, residential address, and facial biometric 
verification photos.

2.2. Financial & Transaction Data: Mobile Money wallet numbers (MTN/Airtel), 
commercial bank account details, transaction logs, wallet balances, investment 
portfolios, and repayment performance history.

2.3. Technical & Usage Data: IP address, device hardware identifiers, operating 
system version, app usage analytics, and login session logs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LEGAL BASIS AND PURPOSES OF PROCESSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We process your personal data under the following lawful bases pursuant to 
Law N° 058/2021:
  - Contract Performance: To onboard your account, process note investments, 
    manage wallet deposits/withdrawals, and collect loan repayments.
  - Legal & Regulatory Obligation: To verify identity (KYC), enforce Sandbox 
    investment caps mandated by the CMA, combat money laundering (AML), and 
    report credit information to licensed Credit Reference Bureaus (CRBs).
  - Legitimate Interest: To maintain platform security, prevent fraudulent 
    transactions, and optimize user experience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DATA STORAGE AND LOCALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In strict accordance with Article 50 of Law N° 058/2021:
4.1. All primary personal, financial, and transactional data are stored securely 
on primary server infrastructure physically localized within the territory of the 
Republic of Rwanda.

4.2. Cross-border transmission or cloud processing of personal data is restricted 
to encrypted channels and executed only with authorized entities maintaining 
equivalent or superior data protection standards certified by the NCSA.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THIRD-PARTY DATA DISCLOSURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rozine does not sell, trade, or rent personal data to third parties. We disclose 
personal data solely to:
  - Regulatory Bodies: Capital Market Authority (CMA), National Bank of Rwanda (BNR), 
    and National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) for regulatory oversight and reporting.
  - Payment Service Providers: MTN Rwanda, Airtel Rwanda, and commercial banks 
    to execute wallet transfers.
  - Credit Reference Agencies: Licensed CRBs in Rwanda for credit scoring and 
    default reporting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under Law N° 058/2021, you possess the following enforceable rights:
  - Right of Access & Rectification: The right to inspect and correct incomplete 
    or inaccurate personal data.
  - Right to Erasure / Deletion: The right to request deletion of personal data, 
    subject to mandatory financial record retention periods prescribed by Rwandan 
    banking and tax laws.
  - Right to Withdraw Consent: The right to withdraw consent for secondary marketing 
    communications at any time.

Requests regarding data subject rights may be submitted to privacy@rozine.rw.
