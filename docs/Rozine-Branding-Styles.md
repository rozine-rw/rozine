07 — LO G O 

## The wing, and the lockup 

The wing mark stands alone in tight spaces — app icons, favicons, avatars. Everywhere else use the horizontal lockup. Never stretch, recolour outside the palette, or add effects. 

















##### 0 8 — R U L E S 

## - Non negotiables 

#### ALWAYS 

- Blue as the primary; other colours identify surface or state. 

- Identical components for identical meaning, on 

- every page. 

- Amounts in full francs with thousands separators where precision matters. 

- The band word before the number, always. 

- Plain language; long explanation behind an ⓘ, not in the layout. 

#### NEVER 

   - A second score competing with the rating. 

   - Gradient backgrounds behind content, or decorative colour without meaning. 

   - Emoji in product surfaces. 

   - "APR", or any annualised rate, anywhere 

   - user-facing. 

   - Any reference to RRA, EBM, TIN or tax compliance. 

   - Language implying guaranteed capital or assured return. 

- Layout with flex or grid and `gap` , never marginspaced inline siblings.
