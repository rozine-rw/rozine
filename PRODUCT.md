# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Investors assess opportunities and track positions; Businesses apply for funding and report performance; Auditors capture and verify evidence; Admin users supervise controlled workflows. Engineering reviewers need clearly identified, synthetic-only demo and UAT surfaces.

## Product Purpose

Rozine is a Rwanda-focused, evidence-led financing marketplace. The BRS and phased implementation plan govern its behavior. Pulse is a non-binding demand simulation, not an investment, approved underwriting decision, or live-money service. Demo and UAT must never be mistaken for production.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, precise. Use the approved brand system and dated amendments, plain-language explanations, and explicit risk and state labels. Reuse the committed interface and assets; this document grants no additional brand, legal, or financial approval.

## Anti-references

Avoid guaranteed-capital or assured-return language, decorative color behind content, emoji in product surfaces, competing scores, and color-only role or state identification. Do not present simulations, unsigned proposals, or test evidence as live or approved results.

## Design Principles

1. Make the environment, role, and current state unmistakable.
2. Distinguish verified facts from simulations and proposals.
3. Give identical meaning identical components across surfaces.
4. Keep consequential actions server-authorized and fail closed.
5. Keep explanations plain and secondary detail out of the primary workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic labels, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, text alongside status colors, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Preserve English, French, Kinyarwanda, and pseudo-localization catalogs. Catalog presence is not launch-language approval or witnessed accessibility acceptance.
