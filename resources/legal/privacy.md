# Rozine Privacy Notice

Version `privacy-2026-11-23` · Effective 23 November 2026 · Issued by Rozine Technologies Ltd (TIN 156642791), Kigali, Rwanda

This notice explains what personal data Rozine collects, why, where it is kept, who sees it and the rights you have. It applies to everyone who uses Rozine: visitors, investors, businesses, Audit Partners and Rozine staff. When you sign up, you tick a box to confirm you have read it. We record the version and the exact time.

## The short version

- We collect only what we need to run Rozine safely and to meet the law.
- Your data is stored on servers in Germany, in the European Union. It is not stored in Rwanda.
- We never sell your data. We share it only with regulators, payment providers and our email provider, as listed below.
- You can see, correct or ask us to delete your data, subject to the records the law makes us keep.

## 1. Who we are and the law we follow

1.1. Rozine Technologies Ltd ("Rozine", "we") decides how and why your personal data is used. We follow Law N° 058/2021 of 13/10/2021 relating to the protection of personal data and privacy in Rwanda.

1.2. Rozine is applying to the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) to register as a data controller and data processor.

1.3. For any privacy question or request, write to info@rozine.rw.

## 2. What we collect

2.1. **Account details.** Your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), language and display settings, and your two-factor sign-in settings.

2.2. **Identity checks (KYC).** Your full legal name, date of birth, address, National ID or passport details, and the identity documents you upload. For an institution or a business: its official name, Tax Identification Number (TIN), registration documents, directors, owners and the name and address of its representative. We do not collect fingerprints or face scans.

2.3. **Screening results.** The outcome of checks against sanctions and politically exposed person lists.

2.4. **Money and investment records.** Your linked Mobile Money (MTN or Airtel) numbers and bank accounts, deposits, withdrawals, wallet balances, commitments, notes, resales, payouts and fees.

2.5. **Business records.** For businesses: bank and Mobile Money statements, monthly figures, photos of the business, the location of its premises, the prospectus, applications and repayment history.

2.6. **Audit records.** For Audit Partners: their licence and certificates, office location, declarations of independence, visit reports and the evidence they record.

2.7. **Your consents.** The version of these documents and of each risk statement you agreed to, and the time you agreed.

2.8. **Messages.** Complaints, support messages and the notices we send you.

2.9. **Technical data.** Your IP address, browser and device details, the devices you sign in from, and security logs of sign-ins and important actions.

2.10. **Waitlist and calculators.** If you join our waitlist or use a calculator on our website, the name, contact details and figures you enter.

## 3. Why we use it

We use your data only on these lawful grounds:

- **To provide the service you signed up for:** opening your account, checking businesses, running raises, collecting repayments, paying out, and handling resales and withdrawals.
- **To meet the law:** identity checks, anti-money-laundering checks, investor limits, keeping financial records and reporting to regulators.
- **To keep Rozine safe:** preventing fraud, securing accounts and investigating misuse.
- **With your consent:** sending you news or marketing. You can withdraw this consent at any time.

Rozine never uses artificial intelligence to make a credit, pricing or investment decision about you. Ratings and offers come from a published formula.

## 4. Where your data is stored

4.1. Rozine stores your personal, financial and transaction data on a server that Rozine rents from Contabo GmbH, in a data centre in Germany, in the European Union. This includes your account and identity details, the documents you upload, your wallet and transaction records, and security logs. Your data is not stored in Rwanda, and Rozine does not keep copies of it in any other country.

4.2. When Rozine sends you an email, your name, email address and the content of that email are passed to our email provider, Resend, Inc., which processes them in the United States to deliver the message. Resend does not store your other data.

4.3. Data travelling between your device, our servers and our email provider is encrypted. Rozine is applying to the NCSA for the registration that Article 50 of Law N° 058/2021 requires to store personal data outside Rwanda.

4.4. Our test site, staging.rozine.rw, holds only test accounts and test data. Its uploaded test files are stored with Cloudflare in Western Europe. Real customer data is never copied to the test site. Please do not upload real documents there.

## 5. Who we share it with

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only with:

- **Regulators and authorities:** the Capital Market Authority (CMA), the National Bank of Rwanda (BNR), the NCSA and the Financial Intelligence Centre, when the law requires it.
- **Payment providers:** MTN Rwanda, Airtel Rwanda and banks, to move money in and out of wallets. The bank that holds the client account will receive the records it needs.
- **Our email provider:** Resend, Inc., as described in section 4.2.
- **Audit Partners:** the CPA assigned to a business sees that business's records so they can verify them.
- **Other users, only where the service needs it:**
  - Investors see a business's name, rating, prospectus and sealed reports.
  - A business sees how much was raised, but never the names of its investors.
  - Resale buyers and sellers do not see each other's names.

## 6. How long we keep it

6.1. We keep your data while your account is open.

6.2. After you close your account, we keep financial and identity records for as long as Rwandan financial, tax and anti-money-laundering laws require, then delete them.

6.3. Ledger entries, consents and sealed audit reports can never be edited. They are kept as permanent records for as long as the law requires.

## 7. How we protect it

7.1. Access to personal data is limited to the staff who need it for their role. Staff need two-factor sign-in, and their important actions are logged with their name.

7.2. If a personal-data breach happens, we will report it to the NCSA within 48 hours and tell the people affected.

## 8. Cookies

Rozine uses only the cookies it needs to work: one to keep you signed in and secure your session, and others to remember your language and light or dark display. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

## 9. Your rights

Under Law N° 058/2021 you have the right to:

- **See your data:** ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct it:** have wrong or incomplete data corrected.
- **Delete it:** ask us to delete your data, except where the law requires us to keep it.
- **Object or withdraw consent:** stop marketing messages at any time, and object to uses based on our legitimate interests.
- **Complain:** contact us first at info@rozine.rw. You may also complain to the NCSA.

We answer every request within 30 days.

## 10. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, for example if we move your data or start using a new provider that receives it, we will publish the new version first and ask you to read it before you carry on using Rozine.
